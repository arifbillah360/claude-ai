About Manhattan Film Festival

Celebrating 20 years of independent cinema in the heart of New York City

EST. 2006

Our Mission

The Manhattan Film Festival is dedicated to discovering, nurturing, and showcasing independent filmmakers from around the world. We champion bold storytelling, diverse voices, and cinematic innovation.

🎬

Discover New Voices

We seek out emerging filmmakers who push boundaries and challenge conventions. Our platform gives first-time directors and seasoned auteurs alike a space to share their vision with passionate audiences.

🌍

Global Perspectives

From intimate documentaries to bold experimental features, we celebrate stories from every corner of the globe. Our festival reflects the diversity of human experience through the lens of cinema.

🏆

Elevate Excellence

Through awards, networking, and industry connections, we help filmmakers take their careers to the next level. Many of our alumni have gone on to major festivals and distribution deals.

20 Years of Indie Cinema in NYC

Two decades of groundbreaking films, unforgettable moments, and a thriving community of storytellers.

2006

The Beginning

MFF launches at Cinema Village with 42 films from 12 countries. The festival's mission: to give independent filmmakers a platform in the heart of Manhattan.

2010

Expanding Horizons

The festival grows to 10 days, adds screenplay competition, and partners with NYU and Columbia Film Schools. Attendance reaches 5,000 for the first time.

2014

Industry Recognition

MFF-premiered films go on to Sundance, Tribeca, and Cannes. The festival becomes an Oscar-qualifying event for short films, cementing its industry reputation.

2018

Digital Innovation

Launch of virtual screening room and filmmaker masterclasses. The festival embraces hybrid formats while maintaining the intimacy of in-person premieres.

2022

Record-Breaking Year

Over 200 films screened from 40+ countries. Sponsored screenings program launches, connecting brands with filmmakers. 15,000+ attendees across 11 days.

2026

20th Anniversary

Celebrating two decades of independent cinema with our most ambitious program yet. New venues, expanded categories, and a special retrospective of past winners. June 12-22, 2026.
