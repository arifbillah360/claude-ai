Get To Know

20th Annual MFF — 2026

The 20th annual Manhattan Film Festival will again be hosted at Cinema Village just steps from Union Square. The famous Cinema Village marquee has been featured in film and television productions including Martin Scorsese's "The Departed" and David Chase's "The Sopranos." Every year we work to program a diverse film lineup featuring the industry's most recognizable names as well as student and emerging talent! We program everything from features, shorts, documentaries, television, web, and new media. MFF has been named by MovieMaker Magazine as one of the "Coolest Film Festivals in the World," and "25 Film Festivals Worth the Entry Fee!"

Always Innovating

Our goal has always been to provide a voice to all filmmakers, while providing opportunities for filmmakers to grow and polish their skills through innovation.
