Pattern Making Excellence

Our pattern making department combines traditional expertise with modern CAD technology to create precise, production-ready patterns. We understand that accurate patterns are the foundation of quality garments.

Using industry-leading software like Gerber and Lectra, our skilled pattern makers develop patterns that ensure perfect fit, optimal fabric utilization, and efficient production processes.

  • Custom pattern development from sketches or samples
  • Complete size range grading (XS to 5XL)
  • Digital pattern libraries for repeat orders
  • Technical specification sheets
  • Fit sample development and adjustments

Precision Cutting Technology

Our cutting department features state-of-the-art automated cutting systems that deliver unmatched precision and efficiency. We utilize computerized cutting machines and laser technology to achieve clean, accurate cuts every time.

Through advanced marker making software, we optimize fabric layouts to minimize waste while maximizing output, ensuring cost-effective production without compromising quality.

  • Automated cutting tables for high-volume production
  • Laser cutting for intricate designs and patterns
  • Fabric spreading and laying systems
  • Cut quality inspection and bundling
  • RFID tracking for production monitoring

Expert Sewing Craftsmanship

Our sewing floor houses over 200 industrial sewing machines operated by highly trained seamstresses with years of experience. We handle everything from basic construction to complex technical garments.

Each production line is specialized for specific garment types, ensuring expertise and efficiency throughout the manufacturing process. Our quality supervisors monitor every stage to maintain consistent excellence.

  • Lockstitch, chainstitch, and overlock machines
  • Flatlock and coverstitch for activewear
  • Bartack and button attachment machines
  • Specialized machines for pockets, collars, and cuffs
  • Skilled operators with an average 10+ years experience
