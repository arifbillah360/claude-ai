Why Submit to MFF 2026?

Our 20th anniversary edition offers filmmakers unprecedented exposure, industry connections, and the prestige of screening in the heart of New York City.

🏆

Oscar-Qualifying

MFF is an Academy Award qualifying festival for short films. Winners are eligible for Oscar consideration in their respective categories.

🌟

NYC Exposure

Screen your work in Manhattan's iconic venues — Cinema Village, Angelika Film Center, and IFC Center — reaching passionate NYC film audiences.

🎬

Industry Access

Connect with distributors, agents, producers, and press. Our Q&A sessions and networking events put you directly in front of decision-makers.

📈

Alumni Success

MFF alumni have gone on to Sundance, Tribeca, Cannes, and major distribution deals. Join a network of 3,000+ filmmakers who launched careers here.

💰

Cash Prizes

Over $50,000 in cash prizes and awards across all categories, plus in-kind services from our industry partners worth $100,000+.

Press Coverage

Rules & Eligibility

Please review our submission guidelines carefully before submitting your film.

📋 General Requirements

  • Films must be completed after January 1, 2023
  • No length restrictions for features or shorts
  • All genres and formats accepted
  • International films must have English subtitles
  • Works-in-progress not accepted

🎬 Premiere Status

  • NYC premiere preferred but not required
  • Films may have screened at other festivals
  • Online/streaming premieres do not disqualify
  • Theatrical releases must be disclosed
  • Student films exempt from premiere requirements

📤 Submission Format

  • Submit via FilmFreeway platform only
  • Upload screener in HD quality (1080p minimum)
  • Include trailer, stills, and press kit
  • Provide filmmaker bio and director statement
  • DCP or ProRes required if selected

⚖️ Rights & Obligations

  • Filmmakers retain all rights to their work
  • MFF may use clips for promotional purposes
  • Selected filmmakers must attend or send representative
  • Submission fees are non-refundable
  • Decisions of jury and programmers are final
